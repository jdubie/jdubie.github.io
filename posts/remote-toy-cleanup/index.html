Cleaning up toys from 7,000 miles away

Can a cheap home robot be run remotely? I built one to find out: a toy-cleanup robot with under $1,000 of parts, a pair of leader-follower arms, and a teleop link that runs over the ordinary internet.

The operator moves a leader arm; the follower arms on the robot copy it, with the joint commands and the camera feed crossing the internet in each direction. Tony Zhao advised on the build.

The point was the cost. If a robot this cheap can be driven from anywhere, a person in one place can do chores in a home on the other side of the world, and every one of those sessions is training data.
