Remote teleoperation at 85 ms glass-to-glass

We built a remote teleoperation system for dexterous manipulation that runs at 85 ms glass-to-glass, with operators 2,000 miles from the robot.

The stack

  • Operator side: a Meta Quest 3S headset and controllers.
  • Robot side: I2RT YAM Pro arms, with an NVIDIA Jetson Orin and GMSL2 cameras.
  • Video: HEVC with hardware encode and decode, plus forward error correction, so lost packets can be rebuilt on arrival instead of waiting for a resend.

“Glass-to-glass” is the full delay from light hitting the robot's camera to the image appearing in the operator's headset: capture, encode, network, decode and display.
