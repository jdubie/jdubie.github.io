Physical Intelligence featured our robot

Physical Intelligence opened a beta of the Pi API on October 13. It lets you upload your own robot data, fine-tune their foundation models on infrastructure they run, and serve the result from a hosted endpoint. Frontier Machines is one of the partners in the launch post.

“Frontier Machines deploys robots at manufacturing companies to automate various different packaging stations. Pi researchers previously trained a policy for Frontier Machines that was sufficiently good for deployment. Within a week of getting access to the Pi API, Frontier Machines trained their own model that matched the policy customized by Pi researchers and subsequently deployed the model at a customer site.”

Physical Intelligence, Frontier Models for Every Robot: Announcing our API beta

The Pi base model is really impressive. Fine-tuned on our own demonstrations, it matched a policy that Pi's researchers had tuned by hand, and we had it running at a customer site within a week.
